Put your AI agents to work in a visual workspace

Big ideas shouldn't require a dozen AI tools or a programming background. Granular brings your models, agents, files, and project context into one visual workspace. Give a boss agent your goal, let it coordinate specialists in parallel, and watch apps, designs, or videos take shape with live previews. Bring your own AI, use built-in terminals and plugins when you need them, and keep each project's memory and files in one place. Go from first plan to published result.
