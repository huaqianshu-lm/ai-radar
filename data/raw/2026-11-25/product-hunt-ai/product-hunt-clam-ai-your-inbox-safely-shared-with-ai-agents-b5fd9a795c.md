Your inbox, safely shared with AI agents

Your inbox is a master key: password resets, bank alerts and health reports all run through it. Clam lets AI agents work in your email without holding that key. You write the rules in plain English, like "Read travel email. Draft replies. Never send." Each agent sees and does only what its rules allow, and you see everything it tries. We start with Gmail. We are building the permission layer for MCP: one that understands what the data means and the context of every request.
