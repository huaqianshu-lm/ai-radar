Claude Code for the rest of us

Working with a coding agent should feel like using a good Mac app, not a terminal. Superagent gives yours a computer: a real browser it drives on your own logged-in sessions, an iOS Simulator it taps and swipes, your files, a board it keeps, routines on a timer. Chats sit in a sidebar you can group, survive restarts, and never steal your focus. Each runs on its own git worktree. Pair your iPhone and the work follows you, encrypted end to end. No account, no server, no second bill. Open source.
