Know when to push your coding agents, and when to ease off

Lancetta is a native macOS menu-bar app that tells you when and how to use Claude Code and Codex. From every quota reading it says what to do next: spend a window about to reset unused, slow down before an agent stops, move to the other agent while it has room. It checks what your agents load at every start, frees the background processes they leave behind, and keeps every session on one wall, the ones waiting on you first. Reading quota spends zero tokens. Free, signed, macOS 15+.
