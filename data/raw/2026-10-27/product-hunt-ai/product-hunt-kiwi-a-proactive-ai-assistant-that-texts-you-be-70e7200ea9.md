A proactive AI assistant that texts you before you ask

Kiwi is your proactive assistant. It keeps track of what's coming up, your trips, plans, bills and the people in them, and texts you at the right moment. When something needs doing, it does it on any app or website when you say yes. Your Saturday is free: want a plan with the kids? Your flight is delayed: want a car for when you land? Your parcel needs a signature while you're at work: move it to Saturday? It all happens in iMessage or WhatsApp. Free during the pilot.
