MiniMax’s Flash model, 3x faster and 70% cheaper for agents

MiniMax M3.1 Flash is MiniMax’s new Flash model for agentic tasks, coding, computer use and cybersecurity. MiniMax says it is about 3x faster online and 70% cheaper for agentic tasks than its predecessor, with output at 200+ tokens per second. Pricing is $0.15 input and $0.60 output per 1M tokens. It’s on the MiniMax API, M Plan and MiniMax Code, and weights are promised later.
