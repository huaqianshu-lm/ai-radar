Every AI coding agent on your Mac, in its place

AI Bridge runs Claude Code, Codex, Grok and OpenCode side by side on your Mac: each the real CLI in a real terminal, on your own subscription. The Grid puts them all on one wall, with cluster and database chats where you need them. Open as many windows as you like, and put agents in Slack that reply when you approve.
