Donating Agent Skills to the Agentic AI Foundation
News today from Anthropic:
We’re committed to keeping Agent Skills open, community-driven, and vendor-neutral. Today, we are donating the standard to the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) under the Linux Foundation,
which we co-founded
with Block and OpenAI last year, and to which we donated the Model Context Protocol (MCP).
I'm pleased to note I called this one
last December
, when Anthropic first declared skills to be an "open standard":
I wouldn't be surprised to see this end up
in the AAIF
, recently the new home of the MCP specification.
