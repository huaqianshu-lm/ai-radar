Agent that drafts contracts, flags risks + gets them signed

Dottr is an AI teammate for contracts that lives in your email. Forward a thread or notes and it drafts the agreement from your templates, reviews risks in plain English, sends it for signature, and chases every signer until it’s done. Signers need no login. It also remembers the terms and dates so you can ask what you agreed later. From draft to signed instantly, so the yes doesn’t go cold.
