NVIDIA’s open multimodal model for specialized agents

Nemotron 3.5 Super VL is NVIDIA’s open, customizable multimodal model for specialized agents. It is a 120B mixture-of-experts model with 12B active parameters that handles text, image, document, and video input, plus reasoning and tool use, in one model. NVIDIA claims up to 5x higher agentic throughput on a single GPU than open models its size. Checkpoints, training recipes, and datasets are on Hugging Face, with a NIM container.
