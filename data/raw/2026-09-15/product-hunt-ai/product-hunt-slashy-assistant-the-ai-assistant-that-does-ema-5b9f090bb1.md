The AI assistant that does email for you

Slashy is an AI-native email client for managing all your inboxes. Slashy Assistant is the AI built into it: it drafts replies in your voice, organizes email, schedules meetings, prepares briefings, and tracks follow-ups. Connect your calendar, CRM, meeting notes, and favorite tools, and it starts helping within five minutes. Use it inside Slashy or through iMessage, Slack, or a phone call. Set up automations in plain English, and Slashy proactively handles recurring work before you have to ask.
