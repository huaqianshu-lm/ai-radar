Find the best deals on products from your agents

Find the best deals on the products you've been asking ChatGPT, Claude, and other agents about. Lists are sorted by best deal, every price is rated against its list price, and any list can be shared with a link. 100% free, in time for Black Friday. Powered by the Parallel Shopping API and Parallel Images API.
