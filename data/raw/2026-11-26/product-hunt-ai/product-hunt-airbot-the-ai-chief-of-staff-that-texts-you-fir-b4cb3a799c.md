The AI chief of staff that texts you first

Most AI assistants wait for you to ask. Airbot texts you first. It checks the conversations, meetings, and customer updates spread across email, Slack, your calendar, and CRM, then brings you only the priorities that matter. Behind every message is work already prepared: a drafted response, a meeting brief, a booking, or a CRM change. Approve what should happen directly in WhatsApp or iMessage. Airbot helps founders and lean teams stay on top of work without living in their notifications.
