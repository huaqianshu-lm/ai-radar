Your AI assistant that actually makes the phone call

Some bookings still only happen by phone, and plenty of us dread making that call. For people who are deaf, hard of hearing, or have speech or anxiety barriers, that call can be impossible. Hai makes it for you. Tell your assistant what you need, approve the plan, and Hai places the call in any language to any business that takes bookings by phone, then reports back in text. Free to start. Built by an indie developer in Japan.
