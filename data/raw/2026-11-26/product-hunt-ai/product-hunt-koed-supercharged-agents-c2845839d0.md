Supercharged Agents!

Koed gives each coding project its own Kin: a customisable assistant that remembers how you like to work, the decisions you’ve made and what you’ve already tried. Your Kin reasons over that context to give Claude Code, Codex and Pi guidance shaped by your project, helping your agents work faster and use fewer tokens. Koed runs on-device and is free and open source. It’s available now for solo developers, with more coding agent integrations, optional cloud processing and team sharing coming soon.
