Amazon's personal AI memory, now on your wrist or phone

Bee Frontier and Bee Eclipse are Amazon's new personal AI devices that help you remember what matters. Frontier is a wearable for your wrist or clothing, while Eclipse attaches magnetically to your phone. Both capture conversations and turn them into useful summaries, reminders, and follow-ups without retaining audio recordings. With Alexa+ integration, your personal AI memory can help you recall important details and act on them later.
