1-1 Live Presentations at Scale with AI.

MyLens is AI for live 1-1 presentations at scale. You add your raw sources, say what matters, and invite your audience. MyLens AI gives every person a live, private presentation, answers their questions using your sources, and create new slides and layers in real time during the presentation. Then the AI tells you how each session went, so you can see who’s doing well, who’s stuck, and what to do next.
