Cloudflare's AI model for fast, cheap data extraction

Clairic is Cloudflare's new AI model for extracting structured information without generating tokens. Give it messy text or images, and it returns typed values like names, dates, amounts, calculations, and estimates in a single pass, with confidence scores. Built for developers who need faster, cheaper data processing than traditional LLM calls. Available in preview on Workers AI at $0.10 per million input tokens, with open weights planned.
