Free, open-source viewer for your own CT, MRI and X-rays

OpenRadView opens the DICOM and NIfTI files from your scan CD or zip on your own computer: linked axial, coronal and sagittal views, experimental 3D and measurements. Optional MCP access for your own AI agent. It is a personal research project for education and has not been clinically validated. Ask a qualified clinician to interpret your scans; do not use the app for decisions about care.
