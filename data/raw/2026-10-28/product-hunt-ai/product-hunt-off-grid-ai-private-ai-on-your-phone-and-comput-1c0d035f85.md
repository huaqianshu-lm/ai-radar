Private AI on your phone and computer.

Off Grid AI runs AI models on the phone and computer you already own. Chat, read documents, ask about photos, talk out loud and make images, with the Wi-Fi off and no account. On your computer it remembers your day: meetings transcribed with no bot in the call, answers that cite their source, and replies it drafts but only sends after your yes. Your phone can use your Mac's bigger models, device to device. Free and open source on iOS, Android, macOS, Windows and Linux.
