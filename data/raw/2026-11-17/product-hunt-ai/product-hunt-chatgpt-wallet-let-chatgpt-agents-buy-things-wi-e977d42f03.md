Let ChatGPT agents buy things without seeing your card

ChatGPT Wallet lets OpenAI's "dots" agents finish purchases for you. The card network gives the agent a payment token, so neither the agent nor the merchant ever sees your card number. You review the items, the total and the card before each purchase, then approve with Face ID or a fingerprint. Built with American Express, Mastercard and Visa.
