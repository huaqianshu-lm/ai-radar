Test your AI agent on working clones of the apps it uses

StateWeave runs your AI agent in sandboxes of the apps it works in: stateful clones of Slack, Gmail, Salesforce, Shopify, Zendesk, Google Calendar, Sheets, Drive, WhatsApp and Teams, with the same APIs, MCP servers and UIs as the real thing. Describe a scenario to fill every app with consistent data, inject faults like outages and 401s, and get graded runs showing which checks failed and the exact calls behind them. Fresh copy every run.
