MakersClaw 2.0 turns a goal into the apps, agents and automations needed to get it done. We originally launched MakersClaw as AI employees that lived in Slack, Teams and Telegram. We rebuilt it around a different idea: instead of hiring an agent for a role, tell MakersClaw what you want done.
