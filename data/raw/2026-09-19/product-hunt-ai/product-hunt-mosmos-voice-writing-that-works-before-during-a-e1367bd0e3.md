Voice writing that works before, during, and after meetings

MosMos goes beyond voice dictation by turning both individual thoughts and group conversations into usable writing. Speak naturally in any app, get fast and accurate text in the style you choose, or ask MosMos to search the web for current information. Its personal glossary remembers specialized terms after you add them once. In multi-speaker meetings, it tracks precise timestamps, distinguishes speakers, and creates structured notes, summaries, decisions, and action items for easier follow-up.
