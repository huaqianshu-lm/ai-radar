One company brain for all your AI agents

Hexis is one company brain for your team and all its AI agents. Through Hexis, your team can align on shared context that's important to align on, non-technical teams can work and collaborate on skills and your team can have hundreds of tools accessible via one MCP gateway. All the information is owned by someone, meaning it's context you can trust, and Hexis enables access control to a single file. It only takes a few minutes to set up, and is free for up to 3 people.
