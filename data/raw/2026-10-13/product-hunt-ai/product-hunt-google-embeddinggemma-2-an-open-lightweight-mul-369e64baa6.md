An open, lightweight multimodal embedding model

EmbeddingGemma 2 is Google DeepMind's open embedding model that maps text, code, images, video and audio into one shared space, so you can search across them from a single query. At 740M parameters it runs on-device, needing about 567MB of RAM on a Pixel 11 Pro for the full multimodal model (191MB for text only). Apache 2.0 licensed, with weights on Hugging Face and Kaggle. MTEB Code score rises from 68.76 to 78.68.
