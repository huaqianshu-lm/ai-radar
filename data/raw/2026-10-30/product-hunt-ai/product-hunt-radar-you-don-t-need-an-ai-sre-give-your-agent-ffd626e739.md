You don't need an AI SRE. Give your agent Radar.

Your agent knows Kubernetes. It doesn't know your cluster. Radar (open-source) gives it context over MCP: ranked issues, a change timeline, logs and owners in one view. In our benchmark of 50 live faults across six tools, Radar found the most root causes. Radar Cloud investigates every alert and posts findings to Slack.
