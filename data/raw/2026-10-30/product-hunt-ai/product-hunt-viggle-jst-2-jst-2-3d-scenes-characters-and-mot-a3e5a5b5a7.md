JST-2: 3D scenes, characters and motion from one model

JST-2 is Viggle’s physics-native world foundation model for generating 3D scenes, characters and full-body motion in one shared world. Scenes take about one second, characters come from one image or text with motion built in, and motion comes from text or video. Try it free in the PINOC browser app, or use the API or MCP with agents like Claude Code. Benchmarks are Viggle’s own blind human tests.
