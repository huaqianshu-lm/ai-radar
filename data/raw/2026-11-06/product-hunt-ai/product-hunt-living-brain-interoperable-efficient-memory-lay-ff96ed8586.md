Interoperable, efficient memory layer for agentic AI

Every AI conversation contains knowledge worth keeping, but most of it gets trapped in separate chats and tools. Living Brain captures that knowledge, connects it to what you already know, and turns it into a persistent memory that keeps evolving. Share that intelligence across AI models, apps, and agents through API, SDK, or MCP, so every new interaction builds on the last instead of starting over. Reuse memory across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, OpenClaw, Hermes, Cursor, Codex and more
