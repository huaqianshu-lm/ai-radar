See who said yes to your team’s AI agents

When an AI agent stops to ask permission, Pushary sends the question to the person running it, in their Mac’s notch or on their phone. Lee’s agent asks Lee. Sam’s agent asks Sam. A rule can send a push to your infra repo to your lead instead. Answers from the phone, Mac app and dashboard are saved with a name, so when something breaks, one page shows whose agent asked and who said yes. Works with Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Agy and Hermes.
