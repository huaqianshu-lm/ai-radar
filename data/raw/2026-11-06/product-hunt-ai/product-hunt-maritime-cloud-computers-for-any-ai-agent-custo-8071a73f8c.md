Cloud computers for any AI agent, customized your way

Maritime gives AI agents their own cloud computers: persistent workspaces, disposable sandboxes, Linux and Windows desktops, and Android phones. Agents can run code, use browsers, and work in apps without APIs, with a human able to take over when needed. Whether you’re building a personal assistant or running thousands of tasks in parallel, Maritime handles the computers so you can focus on what your agent does.
