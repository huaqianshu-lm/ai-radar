Save your AI conversations so they persist after closing tab

Your ChatGPT and Claude conversations disappear the moment you close the tab. With Clipnote, just tell your AI to "save this" and it's kept on the spot — pasting it in works too, of course. It's a place to keep your AI conversations like a notepad, come back to them later, and pick up right where you left off.
