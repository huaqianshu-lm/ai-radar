Check your exposure to critical CVEs with one agent prompt

Zero Wire is a free vulnerability intelligence feed from Cloudflare Radar for security leads and engineers. Unlike CVE databases that list everything, it picks the critical issues in Internet-facing software and explains each in plain language: what's affected, who should check, and what to do now, with links to CVE records, vendor advisories and CISA KEV. Each briefing includes a ready prompt for your AI agent to run a read-only exposure check. No account needed.
