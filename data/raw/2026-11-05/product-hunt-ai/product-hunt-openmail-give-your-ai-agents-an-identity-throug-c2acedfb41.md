Give your AI agents an identity through email

Your agents sign up for tools, receive verification codes and reply to customers, mostly from your Gmail or a shared sending pool where one bad actor gets everyone flagged. OpenMail gives every AI agent its own inbox on your domain with its own sending reputation, for one API call each. Incoming mail reaches your code over webhook or WebSocket, attachments arrive as text, and there's a hosted MCP server and a Hermes plugin. Built and hosted in the EU, free for 3 inboxes.
