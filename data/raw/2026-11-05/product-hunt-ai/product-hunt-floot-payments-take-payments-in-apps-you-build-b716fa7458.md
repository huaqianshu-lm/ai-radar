Take payments in apps you build in ChatGPT and Claude

Floot apps can now take real money. Ask your AI in ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor for "a $9/month plan", "a checkout for this course" or "shipping labels for my orders", and Floot sets it up: subscriptions with a billing page, one-time payments, tickets and deposits, coupons and refunds, plus shipping with live rates, labels and tracking. Runs on Stripe, no API keys or webhooks. Test free in preview, go live when you publish.
