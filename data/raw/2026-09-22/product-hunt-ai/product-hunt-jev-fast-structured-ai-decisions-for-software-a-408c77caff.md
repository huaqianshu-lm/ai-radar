Fast, structured AI decisions for software automation

Jev is TypeSafe AI's System One frontier model: unstructured state in, typed probabilistic decisions out. Parallel sampling delivers ~70-500ms responses, about 20-200x faster and 40-400x cheaper than comparable LLM workflows, with output tokens free. Now available to everyone at console.typesafe.ai with no waitlist.
