Open-source desktop AI agent for any model you choose

OpenPilot is a free, MIT-licensed desktop AI agent. Connect any OpenAI-compatible model (OpenAI, OpenRouter, Ollama, LM Studio), pick a folder, and it reads and edits files and runs commands there. You approve sensitive actions. Windows now, macOS early beta.
