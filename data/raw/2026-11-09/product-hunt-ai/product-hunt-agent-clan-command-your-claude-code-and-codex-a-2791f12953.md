Command your Claude Code and Codex agents with gestures

Agent Clan puts your Claude Code and Codex agents on your screen as a 3D team you run with your hands. Make a fist and they appear. Pinch and pull toward one to pick it, then just say what you need. Hand tracking and voice run on your computer, and everything also works with a mouse. Free on Mac and Windows.
