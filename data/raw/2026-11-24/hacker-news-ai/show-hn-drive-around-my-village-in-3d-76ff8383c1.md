So this started out just over a week ago, could I drive around my village in my old car on the web, and its expanded into a massive map, with races, events, different cars and multiplayer.
I'm a web/app guy, backend guy, and yes I build it with Claude.
I know I shouldn't be astounded by what these Ai agents can do as of today, but here I am still amazed.
I've even got support from a Sim racing guy and built a web driver so he gets full force feedback for his rig, and it works.
Anyway, just a bit of fun!
Uses LIDAR data from the UK Gov + OpenStreetMap data to build the world. Technically, I could do the whole of England/Wales...but the data storage would be huge!
