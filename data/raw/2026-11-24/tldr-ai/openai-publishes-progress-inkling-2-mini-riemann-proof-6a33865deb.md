### Perplexity and Intelligence (9 minute read)

Perplexity measures how well a model predicts the next token on average. Lower perplexity means better predictions. Perplexity has stood the test of time as a metric many research teams have put a lot of effort into optimizing. It will likely play an important role in standardizing how AI progress is discussed as it continues to deliver consistent gains in general intelligence.

### Inkling 2 Mini (9 minute read)

Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Mini, a 26.4B-parameter open-weight model that does extra internal computation to reason more before it answers. It leads every open-weight model under 100B parameters on the DeepSWE coding benchmark.

### AI R&D-Relevant Model Capabilities Are Improving Rapidly (29 minute read)

Frontier AI models often match or beat skilled humans on well-defined coding and optimization tasks. The length of tasks they can complete doubles about every 3.6 months. METR says public evidence can't rule out a drastic speedup in AI research within the next couple of years.

### Tencent Releases Hy4 With Multimodal Input and Million-Token Context (10 minute read)

Tencent's open-weight Hy4 supports text, image, and video understanding with a 770-billion-parameter backbone activating 49 billion parameters per token. It combines million-token context, sparse attention, and a speculative-decoding drafter, targeting coding, computer use, finance, research, and long-running assistant workflows.

### NVIDIA Releases Nemotron-Labs-Sigma Continuous Diffusion Language Models (5 minute read)

Nemotron-Labs-Sigma is a family of 14 open checkpoints at 3B and 8B that generate text by denoising compact token embeddings instead of predicting one token at a time. The 8B model supports few-step generation and self-speculative decoding, reaching a 3.46x average throughput speedup over autoregressive decoding on a GB200.

### Introducing harness adapters: adapt your favorite model to your harness to improve performance and cost (22 minute read)

Hugging Face has introduced harness adapters, allowing models like Qwen3.8-27B to optimize performance in specific harness-task combinations. By incorporating RL-trained LoRA adapters, the models achieve higher accuracy and efficiency, reducing token usage significantly.

### Lean verification of the Riemann Hypothesis (3 minute read)

Levent Alpöge released a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis as a Lean library comprising 17 million lines of code on November 20. The Lean and Mathlib communities have full confidence that Alpöge's proof of the Riemann Hypothesis is correct. Alpöge's library compiles correctly, and the code proves the correct statement. It is expected that the mathematical community will help explain the ideas in the proof in the coming weeks.
