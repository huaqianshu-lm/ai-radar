An AI study agent that remembers where you struggle

Gauth Personal Study Agent connects the questions you snap, the quizzes you take, and the topics you study into one ongoing picture of how you learn. Instead of starting from scratch each session, it spots the gap behind a mistake, builds a short daily plan around it, and adjusts that plan when you miss a review or master a topic early. Practice comes as flashcards, guided step-by-step walkthroughs, and quizzes, with topic mastery and streaks to show progress. Built into the Gauth app.
