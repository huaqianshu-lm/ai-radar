Intent verification for coding agents

Tiden verifies your coding agents built what you actually asked for. Most checks run inside the session that wrote the code, so the tests agree with it by construction. Tiden keeps your intent as requirements that branch and merge like code, and checks every change against that record. The verdict comes from tests run in your environment; no model grades the diff. You decide what merges, and production errors trace back to the release. Works beside Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and Copilot.
