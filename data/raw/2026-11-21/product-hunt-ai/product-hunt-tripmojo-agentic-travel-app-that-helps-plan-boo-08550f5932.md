Agentic travel app that helps plan, book and run your trip

Tripmojo syncs your booking emails into one shared trip, then Mojo, your AI travel agent, helps run it. Plan in a group chat where Mojo answers, runs polls and finds places. On travel day Mojo tries to check you in, saves your boarding pass, flags delays and gate changes, and tells you when to leave. On the trip it keeps today's plan current and splits expenses. Free on iOS and Android.
