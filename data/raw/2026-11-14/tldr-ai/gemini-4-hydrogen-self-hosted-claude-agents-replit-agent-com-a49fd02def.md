### Top Chinese Frontier AI Models Carry the Fingerprint of Export-Controlled NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs (37 minute read)

A forensic analysis of publicly released AI models found evidence that DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, MiniMax, and Ant Group have gained access to Nvidia's Blackwell GPUs. Six models released by these companies since April contain numerical patterns that match Blackwell hardware. Several leading Chinese AI developers have likely begun using Blackwell to train their newest models. While the analysis provides technical evidence of access to advanced computing hardware, it doesn't establish where the chips were located, how access was obtained, or whether export controls were violated.

### Eliminating Nondeterminism in LLM Inference with Integer-Only Arithmetic (15 minute read)

A floating-point number stores a fixed number of significant digits and a separate exponent. When two numbers with very different exponents are added, the small one has to be shifted to line up with the big one, and the digits that fall off on the end are gone. That means that (a+b)+c is not always equal to a+(b+c). This post looks at what happens when integer addition, which is exact, is used.

### How Agent Anomaly Detection scores on benchmarks we didn't write (8 minute read)

Agent Anomaly Detection, tested against external benchmarks, flagged 87% of executed harmful instructions, highlighting strengths in identifying goal hijack and tool misuse but weaknesses with poisoned memory. It demonstrated varying false alarm rates, with the highest at 16% for tasks involving high-impact tools. Ongoing improvements focus on detecting small, potentially harmful actions, distinguishing normal use from misuse, and providing quicker full coverage for new agents.

### Perplexity Decider (26 minute read)

Perplexity has released updates to two models inspired by Jev, TypeSafe AI's System One model. Perplexity Decider supports binary and multi-class classification with ordered or unordered labels. It returns probabilities that applications can use to classify, route, rank, and evaluate inputs. Both new models, Decider v1.3 and Decider Flash v1.3, improve performance and robustness across a broad range of text and vision tasks. They also have a new feature that highlights selected content for named entity recognition, personal information identification, and extractive question-answering tasks.
