Add Claude to your call and get work started as you talk

Claude Meetings lets you add Claude to a Google Meet call as a visible participant. The team can ask questions out loud or in chat, and Claude writes the notes and action items so no one on the call has to. It builds docs, whiteboards, and other artifacts during the call, and teammates on the invite can edit them as you talk. After the call, the notes, transcript, and anything Claude made are on the meeting page in Claude. It is in beta on Team and Enterprise plans, starting with Google Meet.
