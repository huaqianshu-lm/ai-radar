Automations that remember, act, and keep work moving

Computer Automations turns recurring work into reliable, ongoing workflows. Define a task once... such as monitoring sources, compiling reports, updating trackers, or drafting follow-ups... and an agent executes it on a schedule or in response to triggers. Built for professionals, teams, and operations leaders who need continuity without constant supervision, it remembers prior runs, builds on accumulated context, and acts proactively rather than waiting for a prompt.
