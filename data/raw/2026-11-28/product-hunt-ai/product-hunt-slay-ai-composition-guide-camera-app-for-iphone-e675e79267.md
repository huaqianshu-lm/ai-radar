AI composition guide camera app for iPhone

slay is an iPhone camera app that overlays 14 composition guides directly on your live camera view — Portrait 1, Portrait 2, Close Up, Side Chick, Travel Shot, One-Third, Two-Third, Vanishing Point, One-Point Vertex, Two-Point Vertex, Triangle 1, Triangle 2, Aerial Triangle, One-Point Aerial — each with the camera height, angle, zoom and distance to use, plus 38 built-in LUTs, so photos are framed and post-ready without editing.
