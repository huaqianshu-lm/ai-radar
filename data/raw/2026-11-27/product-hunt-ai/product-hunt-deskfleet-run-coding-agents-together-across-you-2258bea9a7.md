Run coding agents together across your team’s machines

DeskFleet gives your team a shared session with the coding agents you already use. Run Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor where your code lives. A teammate can join from their laptop, review the diff, approve the next command, or take control of the remote desktop. Your tools and agent credentials stay on the machine doing the work.
