Private email that puts people who need you first

Kagi Mail is a private email service designed to help you focus on what matters. Its intelligent inbox highlights people waiting for your reply, identifies emails that need action, and keeps newsletters and unwanted messages out of the way. Ask questions about your inbox, organize emails using natural-language rules, and use unlimited aliases and custom domains. No ads, no tracking, no profiling. Includes 50 GB storage and a built-in calendar, with open standards and no vendor lock-in.
