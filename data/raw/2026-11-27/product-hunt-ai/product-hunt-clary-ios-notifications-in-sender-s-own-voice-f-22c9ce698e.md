iOS notifications in sender's own voice from WhatsApp & more

Clary reads your iPhone notifications in the sender's own voice, from WhatsApp, iMessage and more. It uses voice cloning: each person records 20 seconds once, from a link, and agrees first, then Clary makes their voice on your iPhone. Tap a message to hear it in their voice, so you feel closer to your people. It also keeps your notifications as a history on your iPhone, sorted Gmail-style into Messages, Updates and Promotions. Free: history, sorting and 2 plays. Needs iPhone 15 Pro+ and iOS 27.
