The stick figure on your taskbar that answers questions

Doodle is a little AI companion that lives on your Windows taskbar. Ask it anything with Ctrl+Shift+Space, or dictate into any app. When it can't do something, Doodle Evolution builds a bespoke plugin, shows you what it can reach, and waits for your OK.
