Let AI agents sign in for you without seeing your passwords

1Password for Agents lets supported AI agents sign in to your accounts without ever seeing your passwords. When an agent hits a login, it asks 1Password. You approve or deny on your own device, and 1Password fills the password or one-time code directly. Connecting an agent doesn’t open your whole vault. Included in Individual and Family plans, with Meta Muse and Grok Bot rolling out and a waitlist.
