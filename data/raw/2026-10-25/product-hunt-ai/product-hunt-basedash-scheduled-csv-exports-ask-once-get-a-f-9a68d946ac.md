Ask once. Get a fresh CSV in Slack or email every week.

Basedash automations can now send CSV exports. Write it into the instructions — "send a CSV of last week's new signups every Monday" — and each run builds the file from your data. In Slack, the CSV is attached in the message's thread; emails and the automation's run history get a Download CSV link. Downloads go through Basedash, so recipients need access to the automation to open them. Ask for it once. Get the CSV every week.
