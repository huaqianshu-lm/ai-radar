**The details:** 

- OpenAI and Cerebras originally introduced the partnership in January, detailing plans for 750MW of the company’s speed-focused compute.

###### AI RESEARCH

Image source: Anthropic

**The Rundown:** Anthropic published a new study testing how AI agents act while in groups, with its wildest test seeing a shared server devolve into a “turf war” with three Claude agents sabotaging and locking each other out over four hours. 

**The details:** 

- A codebase was given to three hidden Claude agent co-owners, with each assigned a rewrite in a different programming language.
- With no agreed owner or conflict policy, each action was read as hostile, and every Claude generation escalated into sabotage.
- One agent made its software impersonate a rival's to fool a monitoring program, with others locking competitors out or repeatedly stopping their work.
- Peace broke out in some runs, often with a call for human backup, and one agent even apologized and said it “behaved badly with the cloaked daemon.”

**Why it matters:** Agent swarms are the direction a lot of the industry is heading, which makes understanding how they coordinate (and what happens when they don’t) an important area of research. It’s also an urgent one given the string of recent security incidents, with AI agents breaking containment at an alarming rate.

###### COMMUNITY AI WORKFLOW OF THE DAY

Today’s workflow comes from reader Felipe Moreira Ramos:

I built a semi-automated workflow to summarize my live streams. With it, I can record and edit a stream in just one day of work.

Not everyone has time to watch an entire stream, so creating a summary of the main moments is important for people who want to watch it later. Many tools create Shorts from videos by analyzing transcripts, but they are expensive and do not work well for visually heavy content like gameplay. Gemini can analyze both video and audio and has a long context window, so I decided to use Google AI Studio to find important moments.

I send the data to Blender. I create a JSON file containing the AI's response and use a script to add the original video. The script cuts and marks the important moments based on the JSON. The AI's timestamps are not perfect, so I may add or remove sections, or correct a position that the AI identified incorrectly. I then render the video."
