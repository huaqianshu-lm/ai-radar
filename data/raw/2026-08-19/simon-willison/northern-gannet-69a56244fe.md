Northern Gannet, in Pillar Point Harbor, CA, US
This is Morris.
Morris is a local celebrity: the only known Northern Gannet (
Morus bassanus
) in the entire Pacific Ocean.
They showed up in the Farallon Islands off the coast of San Francisco
14 years ago
. They have since made Pillar Point harbor their home, where they are quite easy to spot: the only white bird with a yellow head, usually hanging out with the smaller black Brandt’s cormorants near the harbor sign visible from the end of the commercial pier.
