A blazing-fast, persistent, key-value AI workload database

Thunder is a blazing-fast, persistent, and scalable key-value database built in Rust for modern, data-intensive applications. Designed from the ground up for high throughput, low latency, and efficient resource utilization, Thunder uses a shard-per-core architecture to scale with CPU cores while minimizing contention. Unlike traditional in-memory databases, Thunder combines the performance of modern NVMe storage with intelligent memory management.
