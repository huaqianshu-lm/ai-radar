A modular development board built for agents

Built for Coding Agents, ESP-Mosaico is a modular development board powered by ESP32-S31. It takes AI agents beyond software and into the physical world, enabling them to create, deploy, test, and iterate on real hardware. Expandable modules and multi-unit tiling give AI agents more ways to create, while Chat Coding and Vibe Coding give you intuitive ways to turn ideas into working physical devices.
