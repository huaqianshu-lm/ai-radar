The meal planner that gets all your dishes ready at once

Recipes help you cook one dish. But meals have multiple dishes! Mise schedules every dish backwards from the minute you want to eat: one start time, one running order, a merged shopping list, a cooking mode. That way you get everything ready at the right time and can work from a simple prep list that includes ingredients, instructions, and reminders. Free with no login.
