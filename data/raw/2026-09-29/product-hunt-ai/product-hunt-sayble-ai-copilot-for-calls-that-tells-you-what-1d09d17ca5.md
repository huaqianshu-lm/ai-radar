AI copilot for calls that tells you what to say next

Sayble is a real-time AI copilot for sales calls, client meetings and negotiations. It hears what the other person just said and gives you one clear line to say next, plus a backup, in about half a second. When the call ends, it writes the recap and a ready-to-send follow-up email. Works on Zoom, Meet, Teams and phone calls. Mac & Windows, 10 languages.
