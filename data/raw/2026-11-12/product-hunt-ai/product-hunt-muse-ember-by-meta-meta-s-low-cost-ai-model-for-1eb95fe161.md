Meta's low-cost AI model for high-volume agent tasks

Muse Ember is Meta's smallest multimodal reasoning model, built to handle the repetitive work behind AI agents. From tool calls and coding subtasks to document extraction and routing, Ember delivers efficient execution at scale. It supports text, images, audio, and video, with a 1M-token context window and pricing starting at $0.10 per million input tokens. Pair it with larger models for planning, and let Ember handle the volume. Available through Meta Model API and Muse Code.
