DocSend for AI agents: send and receive docs with context

You send a deck or doc. They ask their AI to read it. The AI sees what's on the page, but not the thinking behind it: why you made those choices, what you left out, or what's still open. Relay shares that context with your document, so they and their AI can understand it and ask questions. You choose what to share. Decisions that need your input, like a discount or a date, come back to you. Try a sample relay. No sign-up needed.
