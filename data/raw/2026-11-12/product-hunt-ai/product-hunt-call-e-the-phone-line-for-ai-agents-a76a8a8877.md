The phone line for AI agents

CALL-E lets your AI assistant make phone calls for you. Ask ChatGPT or Claude to book a table, get a quote, move an appointment or check stock. CALL-E asks for anything missing, shows you the plan, and dials a real US or Canadian number after you confirm. It gets through phone menus, hold and voicemail, then brings back a summary, the transcript and what happened. Builders can add it to Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or OpenClaw with one prompt. New users get 100 free credits.
