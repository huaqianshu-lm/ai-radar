Multi-camera videos with the phones you already own

Most people record with one camera, because more angles feel too hard. Shot.video turns your laptop and phones into a multi-camera studio. Scan a QR code and a phone becomes another camera, with no app to install. Press record once. Every angle records in sync, then AI makes your first edit. It cuts to the camera you're looking at and adds zooms, punch-ins and optional B-roll. Change anything you like. Free to start. No card needed.
