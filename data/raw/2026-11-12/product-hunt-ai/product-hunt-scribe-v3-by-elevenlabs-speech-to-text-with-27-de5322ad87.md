Speech to text with 27% fewer errors in 90+ languages

Scribe v3 is ElevenLabs’ most accurate speech-to-text model, for developers building on calls, meetings, interviews and media. It makes 27% fewer word errors than Scribe v2 across 90+ languages. Unlike transcription that breaks on noise, accents and crosstalk, it labels up to 32 speakers, tags laughter, music and emotion, boosts up to 1,000 of your own terms, detects 56 entity types and handles files up to 15 hours. For live use, Scribe v3 Live runs under 150ms.
