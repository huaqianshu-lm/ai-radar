Your Claude Code and Codex limits, live in the notch

ilha is a native Mac app that lives in the notch (a small pill on other displays) and shows how much of your Claude Code, Codex and Grok Build limits you've used: the 5-hour and weekly windows, when they reset, and whether you're on pace to run out. If you pay for several accounts, it switches the CLI to one with room before the limit, without logging anything out. Insights prices each week at API rates, by project.
