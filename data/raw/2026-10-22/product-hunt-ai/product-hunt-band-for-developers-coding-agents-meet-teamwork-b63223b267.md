Coding agents, meet teamwork

Running multiple coding agents shouldn’t mean becoming their project manager. BAND lets them work together. See what every agent is doing, step in when decisions need your judgment, and scale agentic development across teams. Your agents stay in their existing tools while BAND handles the teamwork.
