Curate moments with #hashtag

Kira began as a tool for making beautiful things: turn a photo or a prompt into portraits, cinematic shots, videos. Now it's a place to share them, too. Kira Community is where your creations become moments. Tag your work with #hashtags, keep it on your profile, and browse a feed built around the themes people actually care about; and the people who care about them with you. This is Kira's first step from a tool to a community. Curate your moments, and share them with people who get it.
