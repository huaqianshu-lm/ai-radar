Web search on a schedule that calls your agent, $2/1k runs

Exa Monitors v2 is web search on a schedule for AI agents. Describe what to watch, pick an interval, and Exa calls your webhook only when something new and relevant turns up, with a summary or JSON output and citations. Per Exa, v2 remembers what it has already sent, is quieter than v1, and costs $2 per 1k runs, down from $15. Watch prices, news, competitors, people and more. Python SDK and API available.
