A fast Mac email app with AI that never leaves your Mac

Most new email apps charge you every month and send your mail to their servers so their AI can read it. Glassmail doesn't. It's a native Mac app for Gmail, iCloud, Outlook and any IMAP account, and its AI runs on your Mac. Ask "what needs me today?" and get an answer. Type "yes, but next week" and get a reply in your own voice. Clear your inbox one key at a time. There's no mail server and no subscription. You pay once, and it's yours.
