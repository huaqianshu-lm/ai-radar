Google’s AI agent that ships fixes and watches the metric

Stitch Loop is an experimental product agent from Google Labs. It connects to your GitHub repo and live product data like logs, user behavior and feedback, proposes fixes, builds them in a cloud sandbox and opens a pull request. Nothing merges without human review. Built-in loops cover security, latency and accessibility, plus custom goals like checkout conversion. It keeps monitoring the metric after launch. Waitlist open worldwide, with priority for eligible Google AI Ultra users.
