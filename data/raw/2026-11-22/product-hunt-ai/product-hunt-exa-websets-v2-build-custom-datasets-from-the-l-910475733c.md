Build custom datasets from the live web, 4x faster

Websets v2 is Exa’s update to its tool for building custom datasets from live web data. Describe what you want in plain language and get rows with sources and reasoning for each match. Per Exa, v2 finds 20% more matches, runs 4x faster and costs about 2x less than v1. The monthly subscription is gone, usage draws from Exa credits, searches are $5 per 1,000 requests, and API access is open to all users on the usage plan.
