Free meeting notes and dictation that stay on your Mac

Zapyso records your calls on the Mac itself, with no bot joining, and writes a transcript and a summary with decisions and next steps. It notices calls in Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Slack and more. Hold the right ⌘ key to dictate into any app. Speech recognition and summaries run on-device, so recordings and notes never leave your Mac. No account. Free, on Mac and iPhone. Needs Apple silicon and macOS 15.
