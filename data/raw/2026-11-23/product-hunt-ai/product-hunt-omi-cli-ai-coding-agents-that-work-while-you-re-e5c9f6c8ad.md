AI coding agents that work while you're away.

Omi can build anything. Give Omi a coding task and let it get to work: it reads your repo, fixes bugs, runs tests, and uses a real browser to check your app. Parallel subagents tackle tasks in separate worktrees. Background agents keep going after you close the terminal; cloud agents can open PRs. Review changes as diffs, undo mistakes, and choose from 13 models or bring your own. Free to start: npm i -g @builditomi/cli
