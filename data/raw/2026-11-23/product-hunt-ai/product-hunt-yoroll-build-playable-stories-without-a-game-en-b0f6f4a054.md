Build playable stories without a game engine

Yoroll lets anyone turn stories into playable, cinematic games without coding or a game engine. Start with a script, image, or video clip, then direct the scenes, add player choices, and create branching storylines with different outcomes. AI helps bring everything together into an interactive experience you can share. It's more like directing your own interactive movie than programming a game.
