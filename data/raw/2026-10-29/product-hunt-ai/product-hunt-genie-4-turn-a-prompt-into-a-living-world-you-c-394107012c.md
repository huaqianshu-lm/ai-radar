Turn a prompt into a living world you can steer

Genie 4 is Google DeepMind’s new real-time world model for creators, game developers and AI researchers. Unlike video generators that hand you a finished clip, Genie 4 builds a world you walk through with your keyboard and steer with text as it runs: change the weather, add a character, set an event in motion. It generates native audio and live dialogue, simulates physics and natural human motion, and uses Gemini to keep scenes coherent. Research preview, waitlist open now.
