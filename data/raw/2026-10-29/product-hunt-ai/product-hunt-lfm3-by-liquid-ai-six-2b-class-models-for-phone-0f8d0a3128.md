Six 2B-class models for phones, laptops and robots

LFM3 is Liquid AI’s third generation of Liquid Foundation Models, with open weights and a shared backbone across text, vision, audio, omni and decision models. The first release is six 2B-class models, including a 1.7B text model that fits in about 1 GB at 4-bit and has a 256K context. Liquid says they run on phones, laptops and edge devices and can be fine-tuned on one GPU. Weights are on Hugging Face.
