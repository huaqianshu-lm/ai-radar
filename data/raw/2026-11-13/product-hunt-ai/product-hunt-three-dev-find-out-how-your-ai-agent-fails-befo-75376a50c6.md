Find out how your AI agent fails. Before your users do.

Almost every team now runs AI in production, but few know how it behaves once real users show up. The failures are quiet: a wrong refund policy, a made-up order status. Nothing crashes, so you hear about it from your users. three.dev finds those failures in your live traffic on its own and ranks them by severity. Setup is one prompt to Claude Code, Cursor, or Codex. Then your coding agent gets what it needs to write the fix.
