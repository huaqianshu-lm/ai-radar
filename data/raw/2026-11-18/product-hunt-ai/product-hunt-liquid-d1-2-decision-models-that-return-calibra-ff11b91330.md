Decision models that return calibrated probabilities

Liquid d1.2 and d1.2-ultra are omnimodal decision models that return calibrated probabilities, so low-confidence answers can go to human review. This version adds audio input and five tasks: reading values, finding text spans, locating objects, estimating numbers with a range, and multi-select. Per Liquid AI, d1.2-ultra scores 75 on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index, two points above the previous best. On the Liquid API now; OpenRouter and Vercel coming soon.
