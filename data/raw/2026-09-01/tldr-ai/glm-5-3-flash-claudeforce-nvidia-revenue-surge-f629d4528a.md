### ChatGPT Now Supports WebMCP (6 minute read)

OpenAI's ChatGPT desktop app's built-in browser and ChatGPT Sites now support WebMCP. ChatGPT and Codex can now use compatible websites' tools instead of guessing its way through the interface. Website teams should design, test, and understand the complete agent experience because users can now be agents, or a person and agent browsing together.

### Intelligent transcription with Gemini 3.5 Transcribe (8 minute read)

Gemini 3.5 Transcribe is a speech-to-text model designed for intelligent voice interactions. It can convert raw audio directly into accurate, polished, and formatted text. Gemini 3.5 Transcribe is available on the Gemini API in Google AI Studio and the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform. The APIs support real-time streaming and pre-recorded audio processing.

### Thinking Machines Lab Co-Founder Barret Zoph Joins Google (3 minute read)

Barret Zoph was a co-founder at Thinking Machines Lab before defecting to OpenAI this year due to a dispute with Chief Executive Mira Murati. Zoph is now leaving OpenAI to join Google as vice-president of research. Google is currently restructuring its AI development efforts to catch up with rivals in developing AI that can create code. Zoph had worked as a research scientist at Google from 2016 through to 2022.

### Anthropic and Nscale strike $45 billion cloud deal (2 minute read)

Anthropic has inked a roughly $45 billion cloud deal with Nscale, a UK-based AI infrastructure company. The facility, which is expected to come online at the end of 2027, will have Nvidia's advanced Vera Rubin chips.
