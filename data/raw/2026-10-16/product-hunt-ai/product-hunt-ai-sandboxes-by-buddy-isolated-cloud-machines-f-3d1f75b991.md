Isolated cloud machines for your AI coding agents

Buddy Sandboxes give AI coding agents their own isolated Linux machines to build, run, test, and fix apps without touching your local setup. Each Sandbox has a persistent disk, root access, Docker, SSH, and a public URL. Create one per task, branch, or PR, then let your agents manage it through MCP, CLI, SDK, or API.
