Let your AI agent design and publish social posts

Most social APIs let an agent publish text or media it already has. PostNitro lets it design the post too. Connect from Claude or ChatGPT in one click (OAuth, no API key), or use our 35-tool MCP server, CLI or REST API. Your agent turns a topic, article or X thread into an on-brand carousel, image post or animated video, then schedules it to LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, Threads, Facebook and Pinterest. Or it hands you an editable draft to finish.
