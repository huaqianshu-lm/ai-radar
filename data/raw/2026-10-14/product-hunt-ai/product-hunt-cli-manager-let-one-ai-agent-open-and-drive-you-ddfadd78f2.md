Let one AI agent open and drive your other CLI agents

CLI Manager now has an AI Control API. Claude Code, Codex or any script can open sessions in CLI Manager, send prompts, wait, and read the screen - in terminals you can watch. Sessions an AI is driving turn green in the sidebar, the orchestrator gets a crown, and Disconnect AI takes control back anytime. Local only (REST + MCP on 127.0.0.1), off by default. Plus everything from launch #1: Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI in one sidebar. Free, MIT open source, macOS.
