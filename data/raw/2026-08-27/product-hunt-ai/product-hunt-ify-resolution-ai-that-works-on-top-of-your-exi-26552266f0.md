Resolution AI that works on top of your existing helpdesk

Most AI support tools ask you to rip out your helpdesk first. Ify works on top of Freshdesk, Zendesk, Salesforce, or HubSpot and gets to work across email, chat, WhatsApp, Slack, and more. The part that usually stalls an AI support rollout is "our docs aren't good enough." Ify solves that by building its own knowledge base: it scrapes your site and docs, generates SOPs from release notes and past ticket resolutions. Built for support teams who want something live now.
