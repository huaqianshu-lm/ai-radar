Assistant for scientific literature and document analysis.

Curie is an AI research assistant built for real scientific work, not a generalist chatbot. It searches PubMed, arXiv, Europe PMC, OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar in parallel, then verifies every claim against the source text, so you see what's backed and what isn't. Point it at a document to extract structured data with the quote behind each value, or run a full systematic review, protocol, screening, PRISMA diagram, with you approving every decision. Projects and a library keep it organized.
