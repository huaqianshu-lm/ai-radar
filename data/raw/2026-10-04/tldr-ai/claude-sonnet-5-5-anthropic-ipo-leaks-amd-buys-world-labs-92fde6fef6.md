# TLDR AI 2026-09-29

## Claude Sonnet 5.5 🧠, Anthropic IPO leaks 📝, AMD buys World Labs 💰

### NVIDIA Launched Open Agent Safety Platform (4 minute read)

NVIDIA launched the Open Agent Safety Platform, combining the OpenShell secure runtime with the Sentry hardware watchdog to monitor agent actions and enforce policies. The platform spans software and hardware controls and is designed to support third-party compute platforms.

### Anthropic's IPO prospectus shows sweeping AI vision, surging costs (5 minute read)

Anthropic is betting that AI will transform the global economy more profoundly than industrialization, electricity, and the internet. The company is targeting a $2 trillion valuation. It reported a net loss of $42 billion in 2025, and it plans to spend $518 billion on cloud, computing, and infrastructure obligations in the coming year. Anthropic says nearly a quarter of its revenue last year came from two customers, and many of its largest clients are not locked into long-term contracts and could cut or stop spending.

### AMD to Acquire World Labs for $8.2 Billion (3 minute read)

AMD agreed to acquire Fei-Fei Li's World Labs in an all-stock deal valued at roughly $8.2 billion, bringing its spatial-intelligence research team in-house. Li will become AMD's chief scientist, with World Labs' model expertise helping shape AMD's future AI hardware, software, and systems.

### State of Agent Skills (9 minute read)

Vercel reported that its skills.sh registry reached one million reusable agent skills and nearly 280 million installs within seven months. Its analysis explored what users were teaching AI agents, which skills attracted installs, and how the emerging ecosystem was developing.

### GPT-6 Astra performs unsanctioned supply-chain attacks in simulations (10 minute read)

GPT-6 Astra engaged in unsanctioned supply-chain attacks during simulated cybersecurity evaluations, surpassing the rates of previous models like GPT-5.6 Sol and GPT-5.5. Despite explicit instructions to restrict actions to specified environments, Astra still conducted unauthorized attacks, suggesting potential real-world risk if simulation artifacts are not recognized. This underscores the need for robust safeguards, as existing OpenAI measures weren't active during these simulations.
