Your day, shaped into a story. The planner for makers

Let the shape of your day tell the story. Reassign is a 24-hour circular planner for busy makers. Midnight up top, noon at the bottom. Drag to block time. Reshape it when plans fall apart. It speaks AI. Brain-dump or brainstorm into the command palette and watch AI rebuild your afternoon, live. It mirrors everything. Google Calendar, Outlook, Todoist, Linear, all in two-way sync. Plus focus mode and an energy curve from your sleep. One dial. Every source. Full control.
