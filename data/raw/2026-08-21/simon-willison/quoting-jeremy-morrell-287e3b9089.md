My hypothesis is that
there is a new opportunity for Extensible Software on the web
. LLMs radically lower the cost of authoring extensions, and modern sandbox primitives lower the deployment cost and provide good security boundaries. We can build our app as a solid, accountable core, and allow users to safely extend it in many directions by having LLMs fill in the missing pieces.
We can give our users super powers.
—
Jeremy Morrell
, Extensible Software in the age of LLMs
