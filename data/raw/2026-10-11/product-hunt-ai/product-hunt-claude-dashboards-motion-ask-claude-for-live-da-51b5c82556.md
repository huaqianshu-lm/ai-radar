Ask Claude for live dashboards and animated explainers

Claude Dashboards and Claude Motion are beta artifacts in Claude. Dashboards connects to BigQuery, Databricks, Snowflake, Redshift, ClickHouse or Salesforce and turns a plain language question into a live dashboard that refreshes as your data changes, with the query behind every number. Motion turns reports, charts and walkthroughs into short animations written as code, so you can edit any word, number or timing, then export an MP4. Dashboards is on paid plans, Motion on Team and Enterprise.
