Real-time decisions for voice and video agents

Sense is a real-time decision model for voice and video agents. It runs on streaming audio and video and answers your questions about the conversation many times per second: is the user done speaking, did they just interrupt, should the agent stop talking. You write the questions, Sense returns the decisions, and your agent acts on them. Build with the SDK for real-time sessions, or send single requests to explore. New accounts start with 1,000 in free credits.
