The visual builder for agentic workflows

Mermaid Flow lets you build agentic workflows as diagrams, then run them. Think issue triage, weekly recaps, PR reviews: work you'd otherwise hand to a long prompt or a skill file. Describe what you want, start from a template, or bring the file, and you get a workflow with every step laid out. Connect Github, Slack, Notion, and more in one click, then hit run or put it on a schedule. Every run is saved step by step, so you can see what happened and your team can follow along. Free to start.
