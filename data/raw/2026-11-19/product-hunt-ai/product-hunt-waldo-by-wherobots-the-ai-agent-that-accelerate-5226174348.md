The AI agent that accelerates engineering with spatial data

Waldo is the geospatial data agent that turns questions about the physical world into answers, maps, and production-ready data pipelines. When the work needs to run continuously, Waldo turns it into a scheduled pipeline on your existing data platform. Built for spatial analytics at scale by the creators of Apache Sedona.
