Fast, low-cost model from $0.07/M input

Hy4 flash is Tencent’s most cost-effective Hy model to date, with 224B total parameters, 12B active, multimodal input and a 1M-token context. Per Tencent, it decodes above 150 tokens per second and costs about $0.07 per million input tokens and $0.28 output. It targets everyday tasks like document work, slides, deep research and computer use, while Hy4 handles harder engineering. Open-sourcing is planned, but the weights are not out yet.
