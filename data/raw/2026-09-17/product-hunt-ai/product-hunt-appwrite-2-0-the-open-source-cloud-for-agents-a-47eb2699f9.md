The open-source cloud for agents and developers

Appwrite 2.0 is the second major generation of the platform. It replaces the engine underneath, rebuilds the Console on top, and expands what a project can hold: relational, schemaless, and vector data, native PostgreSQL and MySQL engines, S3-addressable storage, an OAuth 2.1 and OIDC-compliant identity provider, and the network layer in front of all of it.
