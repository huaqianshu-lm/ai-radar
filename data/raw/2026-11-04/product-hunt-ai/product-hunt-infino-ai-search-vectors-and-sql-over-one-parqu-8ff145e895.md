Search, vectors and SQL over one Parquet copy, for agents

Infino is an open-source retrieval engine for AI agents. It keeps one copy of your data as Parquet on S3 or local disk, with BM25 and vector indexes inside the files. Agents run keyword, vector, hybrid and SQL search on it. Apache-2.0. Infino Cloud is generally available, with a free tier.
