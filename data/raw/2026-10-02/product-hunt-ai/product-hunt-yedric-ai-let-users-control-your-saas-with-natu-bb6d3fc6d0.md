Let users control your SaaS with natural language

Your users shouldn't have to learn where every feature lives. Yedric adds an embeddable AI agent to your SaaS that turns natural-language requests into actions your product already knows how to perform. Users simply say what they want done and Yedric handles the interaction. Instead of building and maintaining your own agent experience, developers can make their existing product AI-native in under 30 minutes.
