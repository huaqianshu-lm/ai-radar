Point at your app and your AI agent changes the code

Ilse puts a toolbar on top of the React app you already have running. Click an element and say what should change, drag to reorder, resize, or tweak spacing and colors with your project's own tokens. Simple tweaks land instantly, with no AI. The rest goes to the agent you already use (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini), pointing at the right spot in the code. Pro adds test mode: Ilse walks real user journeys on her own and reports what breaks.
