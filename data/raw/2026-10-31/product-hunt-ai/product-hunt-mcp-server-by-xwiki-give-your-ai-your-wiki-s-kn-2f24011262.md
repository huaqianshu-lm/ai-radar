Give your AI your wiki's knowledge while keeping the control

XWiki MCP Server is an open-source, self-hostable extension that connects XWiki to the AI agent of your choice: OpenCode, Pi, Codex, Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, and more. You can already use the extension to query your knowledge base to get answers, create and edit pages, and work with structured data directly in XWiki.
