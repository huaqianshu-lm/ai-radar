Run a team of AI agents from a pixel-art office

Most agent tools are a chat box. Pixel Office is a place: each employee is a real AI agent (Claude, Codex, Gemini or OpenRouter) with its own role, memory, skills and permission level. See at a glance who is working, who needs your approval and who is done. Hire in a character creator, hand out tasks on a board, let agents message each other, and decorate the office with coins from finished work. Open source, one command, runs on your machine with your own keys.
