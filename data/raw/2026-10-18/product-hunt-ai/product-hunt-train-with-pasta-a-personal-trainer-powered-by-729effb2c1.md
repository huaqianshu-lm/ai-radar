A personal trainer powered by Apple Intelligence

Pasta is a personal trainer in your pocket. Ask for "a 30 minute upper body workout", and it'll build it for you. While you train, the app uses voice as a guide, so you can focus on pushing yourself hard. It does plans too! Tell it what you're training for and it writes a plan around the days you can train. The app runs on Apple Intelligence and needs no account. Your progress and health data stays on your device. Requires an iPhone with Apple Intelligence on iOS 27.
