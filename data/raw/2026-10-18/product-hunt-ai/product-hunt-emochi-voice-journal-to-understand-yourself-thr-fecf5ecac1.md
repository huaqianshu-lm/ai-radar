Voice journal to understand yourself through art and science

Emochi turns your voice notes into a living, generative character that reflects your emotional world. Vent about your day, untangle a thought, or check in with yourself. Your words are transcribed instantly, helping you notice patterns in how you feel. Built by a doctor, a designer, and two AI engineers, Emochi brings affective science and playful art to private, non-clinical self-reflection. Start with a voice note. Meet your Mochi.
