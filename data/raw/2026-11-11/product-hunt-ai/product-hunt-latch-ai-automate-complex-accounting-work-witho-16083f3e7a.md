Automate complex accounting work without a single prompt

Latch is built for accountants buried in click-heavy close work. Instead of writing prompts, you do a task on screen and talk it through, including the parts no SOP captures: the three-day matching window, the rate you always override. Latch asks about the exceptions, then builds a sidekick that runs the task the same way every time, right in the tools you already use. When something doesn't fit, it stops and asks you. Every step is logged for review.
